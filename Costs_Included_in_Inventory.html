Problem

Swannanoa Corporation

Swannanoa Corporation is a wholesale distributor of truck replacement parts. Initial amounts taken from Swannanoa's records are as follows:

Inventory at December 31 (based on a physical count of goods in Swannanoa's warehouse on December 31): $1,260,000

VendorTermsAmount

Additional information:

  1. Parts held by Swannanoa on consignment from Canton, amounting to $160,000, were included in the physical count of goods in Swannanoa's warehouse and in accounts payable at December 31.
  2. Parts totaling $23,000, which were purchased from Franklin and paid for in December, were sold in the last week of the year and appropriately recorded as sales of $29,000. The parts were included in the physical count of goods in Swannanoa's warehouse on December 31 because the parts were on the loading dock waiting to be picked up by customers.
  3. Parts in transit on December 31 to customers, shipped f.o.b. shipping point on December 28, amounted to $36,000. The customers received the parts on January 6 of the following year. Sales of $42,000 to the customers for the parts were recorded by Swannanoa on January 2.
  4. Retailers were holding goods on consignment from Swannanoa which had a cost of $220,000 and a retail value of $260,000.
  5. Goods were in transit from Grandfather to Swannanoa on December 31. The cost of the goods was $26,000, and they were shipped f.o.b. shipping point on December 29.
  6. A freight bill in the amount of $2,100 specifically relating to inventory purchased in December, all of which was still in the inventory at December 31, was received on January 3. The freight bill was not included in either the inventory or in accounts payable at December 31.
  7. All the purchases from Bakersville occurred during the last seven days of the year. These items have been recorded in accounts payable and accounted for in the physical inventory at cost before discount. Swannanoa's policy is to pay invoices in time to take advantage of all discounts, adjust inventory accordingly, and record accounts payable net of discounts.
Required: Complete the schedule of adjustments to the initial amounts.

Every one of the seven items gets a figure in all three columns — enter 0 where an item does not affect that column. Enter reductions as negative numbers. Work through each item by asking who held title at December 31 and whether the physical count already picked the goods up.
