Problem

Whiteside Corporation

Listed below are the transactions that affected the shareholders' equity of Whiteside Corporation during the period 2027–2029. At December 31, 2026, the corporation's accounts included:

Account($ in thousands)
  1. On November 1, 2027, the board of directors declared a cash dividend of $0.70 per share on its common shares, payable to shareholders of record November 15, to be paid December 1.
  2. On March 1, 2028, the board declared a property dividend consisting of corporate bonds of Cheoah Corporation that Whiteside was holding as an investment. The bonds had a fair value of $2.0 million, but were purchased two years previously for $1.8 million. Because they were intended to be held to maturity, the bonds had not been previously written up. The property dividend was payable to shareholders of record March 13, to be distributed April 5.
  3. On July 12, 2028, the corporation declared and distributed a 6% common stock dividend, when the market value of the common stock was $24 per share. Cash was paid in lieu of fractional shares representing 360,000 equivalent whole shares.
  4. On November 1, 2028, the board declared a cash dividend of $0.70 per share on its common shares, payable to shareholders of record November 15, to be paid December 1.
  5. On January 15, 2029, the board declared and distributed a 3-for-2 stock split effected in the form of a 50% stock dividend, when the market value of the common stock was $25 per share. Use the Paid-in capital—excess of par account when recording the entry instead of Retained earnings.
  6. On November 1, 2029, the board declared a cash dividend of $0.55 per share on its common shares, payable to shareholders of record November 15, to be paid December 1.
Required:
  1. Prepare the journal entries that Whiteside recorded during the three-year period for these transactions.
  2. Prepare comparative statements of shareholders' equity for the three-year period. Net income was $360 million, $425 million and $485 million for 2027, 2028 and 2029, respectively.

Enter every answer in thousands — $2.0 million is entered as 2,000 and $360 million as 360,000.
