Problem

Tuckasegee Wholesale Beverage Company

Tuckasegee Wholesale Beverage Company regularly factors its accounts receivable with the Balsam Finance Company. On April 30, 2027, the company transferred $810,000 of accounts receivable to Balsam. Balsam remits 90% of the factored amount and retains 10%. When Balsam collects the receivables, it remits to Tuckasegee the retained amount less a 5% fee (5% of the total factored amount). Tuckasegee estimates the fair value of the last 10% of its receivables to be $61,000.

Required: Prepare the journal entry for Tuckasegee Wholesale Beverage for the transfer of accounts receivable on April 30 under each of the following three assumptions.
  1. The transfer is made without recourse and the sale criteria are met.
  2. The transfer is made with recourse, the fair value of the recourse obligation is estimated to be $6,000, and the sale criteria are met.
  3. The transfer is made without recourse and the sale criteria are not met.

Requirement 3 — Without recourse, sale criteria not met

The transfer is accounted for as a secured borrowing: the receivables stay on Tuckasegee's books and the cash received is a liability. Record only the cash received and the related liability at the transfer date — no receivable from the factor and no loss. Balsam's fee is recognized as interest expense as the receivables are collected.
