Segmented Income Statements by Territory and by Product — Nantahala Outdoor Supply Co.

Breaking a companywide contribution format income statement down first by sales territory, then breaking the Asheville Territory down further by product line, to separate traceable fixed expenses from fixed expenses that are common to the segments above them.

Before you start: a cost is traceable to a segment only if it would disappear if that segment were eliminated; everything else is common to the segments above it and should not be allocated down. Segment margin is contribution margin minus that segment's own traceable fixed expenses — common fixed expenses are subtracted only once, at the bottom, from the total. Enter all dollar amounts in whole dollars.
