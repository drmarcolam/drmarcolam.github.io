How to use the template: every transaction below changes exactly two places in the accounting equation (Cash Asset + Noncash Assets = Liabilities + Contributed Capital + Earned Capital), so each row has two effects. Pick the statement element and account for each effect, whether it increases or decreases, and the dollar amount. When a transaction also touches the income statement, fill in the revenue or expense effect too — net income flows straight into Earned Capital, so it should match one of your two balance sheet effects.
