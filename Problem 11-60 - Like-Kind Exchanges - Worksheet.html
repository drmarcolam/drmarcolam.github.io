Reference: like-kind exchange rules (§1031)

A like-kind exchange of real property held for productive use in a trade or business or for investment defers the gain. A trade that does not qualify is a taxable sale.

Amount realized and gain or loss realized
  • Amount realized = fair market value of the property received + boot received (cash or non-like-kind property) − boot paid.
  • Gain or loss realized = total amount realized − adjusted basis of the property given up.

Gain (loss) realized = total amount realized − adjusted basis

Gain or loss recognized
  • Not a like-kind exchange: the entire realized gain or loss is recognized.
  • Like-kind exchange: gain is recognized only to the extent of boot received, and never more than the gain realized.
  • Boot paid does not trigger recognition.
  • A realized loss on a like-kind exchange is never recognized, even when boot is received.

Gain recognized = lesser of (gain realized, boot received)

Basis in the property received
  • Taxable exchange: basis = fair market value (cost) of the property received.
  • Like-kind exchange: basis = fair market value of the property received − deferred gain (or + deferred loss).
  • Equivalently: basis = adjusted basis given up + boot paid + gain recognized − boot received.

Deferred gain (loss) = gain (loss) realized − gain (loss) recognized
