How to classify: a balance sheet (B) item is a balance outstanding at a point in time — something still owed or still carried on the books. An income statement (I) item is an amount earned or incurred during the year — a gain, a loss, or an expense that flows through net income. Every account below belongs to exactly one of the two statements.
