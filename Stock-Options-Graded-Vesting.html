Saluda Brands

Saluda Brands is a calendar-year firm. As part of its executive compensation plan, at January 1, 2027 the company issued 400,000 executive stock options permitting executives to buy 400,000 shares of Saluda stock for $34 per share. One-fourth of the options vest in each of the next four years, beginning at December 31, 2027 (graded vesting).

Saluda elects to separate the total award into four groups (tranches) according to the year in which they vest, and measures compensation cost for each vesting date as a separate award.
