An accounting intern segmented a merchandiser's income statement using the absorption format and allocated common fixed expenses down to two divisions. Work out what she did, redo the statement in contribution format, and use it to find break-even points for the company and each division — before and after a change in how sales staff are paid.

Before you start: Toxaway's only variable selling and administrative expense is a sales commission. A traceable fixed expense would disappear if its division were dropped; a common fixed expense would not, and allocating it to divisions can make a segment look less profitable than it really is. When you compute a division's break-even point in dollar sales, use only that division's own traceable fixed expenses in the numerator — not any allocated common cost. Enter dollar amounts in whole dollars and round break-even dollar sales to the nearest dollar.

Required:
