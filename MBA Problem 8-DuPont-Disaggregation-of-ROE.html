(c) Calculate the profitability and productivity components of ROA. (d) Confirm the full DuPont disaggregation: ROE = PM × AT × FL. Note: the company has no noncontrolling interest.

Only one year of income is given, so every ratio uses the current year’s net income or revenue over an average balance drawn from the two balance sheets.

(a) Return on equity

ROE = net income ÷ average stockholders’ equity.

(b) First disaggregation: ROE = ROA × FL

ROA = net income ÷ average total assets. Financial leverage = average total assets ÷ average stockholders’ equity. Notice that average total assets cancels when the two are multiplied — which is why the identity holds.
