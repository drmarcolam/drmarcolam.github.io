Problem

Tuckasegee Company

Tuckasegee Company has used the LIFO method of accounting for inventory during its first two years of operation, 2025 and 2026. At the beginning of 2027, Tuckasegee decided to change to the average cost method for both tax and financial reporting purposes. The income tax rate for all years is 25%.

Tuckasegee issued 44,000 $1 par common shares for $200,000 when the business began, and there have been no changes in paid-in capital since then. Dividends were not paid the first year, but $11,000 cash dividends were paid in both 2026 and 2027.
