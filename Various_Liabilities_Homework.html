Camden Biotechnology

Camden Biotechnology began operations in September 2027. The following selected transactions relate to liabilities of the company for September 2027 through March 2028. Camden's fiscal year ends on December 31. Its financial statements are issued in April.

2027

  1. On September 5, opened checking accounts at Second Commercial Bank and negotiated a short-term line of credit of up to $33,000,000 at the bank's prime rate (15.5% at the time). The company will pay no commitment fees.
  2. On October 1, borrowed $30 million cash from Second Commercial Bank under the line of credit and issued a five-month promissory note. Interest at the prime rate of 15% was payable at maturity. Management planned to issue 10-year bonds in February to repay the note.
  3. Received $3,300 of refundable deposits in December for reusable containers used to transport and store chemical-based products.
  4. For the September–December period, sales on account totaled $5,020,000. The state sales tax rate is 3% and the local sales tax rate is 3%. (This is a summary journal entry for the many individual sales transactions for the period.)
  5. Recorded the adjusting entry for accrued interest.

2028

  1. In March, paid the entire amount of the note on its March 1 due date, using proceeds from a February issuance of $28.0 million of 10-year bonds at face value, along with other available cash.
  2. The storage containers covered by refundable deposits are expected to be returned during the first nine months of the year. Half of the containers were returned in March 2028.

1. Journal Entries

Prepare the appropriate journal entries for items a–g.

Choose each account from the chart-of-accounts dropdown in the Account column.

(a) Line of credit — No entry required. Negotiating a line of credit is a memorandum-only event; no cash or liability changes hands until the company actually borrows against it.

(b) October 1, 2027 — Issuance of the Promissory Note

Record the $30 million borrowed under the line of credit.

AccountDebitCredit

(c) December 2027 — Refundable Container Deposits

Record the deposits received for the reusable containers.

AccountDebitCredit

(d) September–December 2027 — Sales on Account

Record the summary entry for sales on account, including state and local sales tax (3% + 3% = 6%).

AccountDebitCredit

(e) December 31, 2027 — Accrued Interest

Accrue interest on the note for the three months since issuance (October 1 – December 31, 2027).

AccountDebitCredit

(f-i) February 2028 — Issuance of 10-Year Bonds

Record the bond issuance used to help repay the note.

AccountDebitCredit

(f-ii) March 1, 2028 — Payment of the Note at Maturity

Record payment of the note, the interest already accrued at December 31, and the remaining two months of interest (January – February).

AccountDebitCredit

(g) March 2028 — Return of Half the Containers

Refund the deposits for the containers returned.

AccountDebitCredit
