Problem

Cullowhee Foods Company and Balsam Mills, Inc.

Cullowhee Foods Company (CFC) and Balsam Mills, Inc. (BMI) compete in the consumer packaged goods sector, both from the mountains of western North Carolina. Refer to the following financial data for the two companies to answer the requirements.

$ millionsCFCBMI
Required: (a) Compute days inventory outstanding, days sales outstanding, days payables outstanding and the cash conversion cycle for both companies. (b) Which company uses inventory more efficiently? (c) Which company better manages its accounts receivable? (d) Which company better manages its accounts payable?

DIO = average inventory ÷ cost of sales × 365

DSO = average accounts receivable ÷ total revenue × 365

DPO = average accounts payable ÷ cost of sales × 365

CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO

Note which denominator each measure takes. Inventory and payables are both measured against cost of sales, because that is the flow they belong to; receivables are measured against revenue, because that is what customers were billed.
