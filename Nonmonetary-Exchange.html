Highland Ridge Company and Foothills Company

Highland Ridge Company owns a building that it leases to others. The building's fair value is $1,750,000 and its book value is $1,080,000 (original cost of $2,350,000 less accumulated depreciation of $1,270,000). Highland Ridge exchanges this building for one owned by Foothills Company.

The building's book value on Foothills’ books is $1,230,000 (original cost of $1,950,000 less accumulated depreciation of $720,000). Foothills also gives Highland Ridge $175,000 cash to complete the exchange. The exchange has commercial substance for both companies.

Preliminary step. Determine the fair value of the building Foothills gives up.
