The Allowance Method

Combining a percentage-of-credit-sales accrual during the year with an aging-of-receivables adjustment at year-end, and presenting receivables on the balance sheet.

Rounding convention: do not round intermediate calculations. Round every dollar figure to the nearest whole dollar. Enter reductions (write-offs, contra accounts) as negative numbers where the worksheet says so.
Problem

Waynesville Clothing Corporation

Waynesville Clothing Corporation grants its customers 30 days' credit. The company uses the allowance method for its uncollectible accounts receivable. During the year, a monthly bad debt accrual is made by multiplying 3% times the amount of credit sales for the month. At the fiscal year-end of December 31, an aging of accounts receivable schedule is prepared and the allowance for uncollectible accounts is adjusted accordingly.
