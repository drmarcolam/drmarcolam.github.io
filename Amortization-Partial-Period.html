Sylva Corporation

On June 30, 2027, Sylva Corporation completed the acquisition of Cashiers Corporation for $1,880,000 in cash. The fair value of Cashiers’ net identifiable assets was $1,600,000.

Included in the assets purchased was a patent valued at $76,800. The remaining legal life of the patent was 13 years, but Sylva believes the patent will only be useful for another 8 years.

Sylva also acquired a franchise on October 1, 2027, by paying an initial franchise fee of $192,000. The contractual life of the franchise is 10 years.

Preliminary step. Determine goodwill, the annual amortization rate for each intangible, and the partial-year amortization for 2027.
