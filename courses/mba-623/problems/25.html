Classifying Costs and Decision-Making

Sorting a job's costs by how they behave (variable or fixed) and how they trace to the job (direct or indirect), then using that classification to judge what a bid should cover.

How to classify: ask two separate questions about each cost. Does its total change with how much work is done (variable), or does it stay the same regardless (fixed)? And can it be traced specifically to the Black Mountain Town Hall project (direct), or is it shared across all of the company's projects (indirect)? A cost can be any combination of the two.
