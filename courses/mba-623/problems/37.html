Problem

Blue Ridge Aerosystems, Inc.

Blue Ridge Aerosystems, Inc., an aeronautics subcontractor headquartered in Hendersonville, North Carolina, designs and manufactures electronic control systems for commercial airliners. The vast majority of all commercial aircraft are manufactured by Boeing in the U.S. and Airbus in Europe; however, there is a relatively small group of companies that manufacture narrow-body commercial jets. Assume for this exercise that Blue Ridge does contract work for the two major manufacturers plus three companies in the second tier.

Because competition is intense in the industry, Blue Ridge has always operated on a fairly thin 20% gross profit margin; hence, it is crucial that it manage nonmanufacturing overhead costs effectively in order to achieve an acceptable net profit margin. With declining profit margins in recent years, Blue Ridge Aerosystems' CEO has become concerned that the cost of obtaining contracts and maintaining relations with its five major customers may be getting out of hand. You have been hired to conduct a customer profitability analysis for fiscal 2027.

Blue Ridge Aerosystems' nonmanufacturing overhead consists of $2,000,000 of general and administrative (G&A) expense (including, among other expenses, the CEO's salary and bonus and the cost of operating the company's corporate jet) and selling and customer support expenses of $3,150,000 (including 5% sales commissions and $750,000 of additional costs).
