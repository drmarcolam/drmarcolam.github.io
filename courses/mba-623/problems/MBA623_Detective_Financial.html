Required

Identify, correct, and analyze

  1. Identify and explain each of the six issues.
  2. For Issues A through E, show the required correction using the FSET and state which draft balances are overstated or understated.
  3. Prepare corrected 2026 net sales, operating expenses, operating income, accounts receivable net, inventory, PP&E net, accrued liabilities, deferred revenue, and total assets. Do not update income tax in this exercise.
  4. Reclassify the cash flows in Issue F. Compute corrected cash from operating, investing, and financing activities. The net change in cash must remain the same as the draft.
  5. Calculate 2026 gross margin, receivables turnover, days sales outstanding, inventory turnover, days inventory outstanding, debt to equity, and operating cash flow to debt using average balances where applicable.
  6. Write a 250 to 350 word email to the CFO identifying the three most important concerns, at least one plausible nonproblematic explanation, and the additional information you would request.
