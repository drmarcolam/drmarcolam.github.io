Problem

Blue Ridge InkWorks

Blue Ridge InkWorks, headquartered in Asheville, North Carolina, produces color ink cartridges for inkjet printers. Cartridges are sold to mail-order distributors for $5.20 each.

Cost and volume data

ItemAmount
    Required: A supplier has offered a one-year contract to supply ink for the cartridges (including shipping). Determine the effect of accepting this offer on annual profit.

    Work through it in steps

    Only the costs that actually change with this decision belong in the analysis. The selling price, current sales volume, annual capacity, and total fixed costs are given for context, but none of them change if the offer is accepted, so none of them belong in your calculation.

    ItemAmount
    (a) Current variable manufacturing cost per unit
    (b) Variable cost savings per unit if the offer is accepted
    (c) Net change in cost per unit — amount
    (c) Net change in cost per unit — direction
    (d) Effect on annual production costs — amount
    (d) Effect on annual production costs — direction
    (e) Additional rental income for the year
    (f) Net effect on annual profit — amount
    (f) Net effect on annual profit — direction
    (g) Recommendation

    The direction in (f) and the recommendation in (g) always agree: if accepting the offer would decrease annual profit, management should reject it; if it would increase annual profit, management should accept it.
