Determining Sales for a Desired After-Tax Profit

Grossing an after-tax profit target up to the pretax income needed to earn it, then solving the CVP equation for the unit sales that will produce it.

Problem

Nantahala Container Corp.

Assume Nantahala Container Corp., based in the Nantahala region of western North Carolina, produces 16-ounce beverage containers. Further assume that the company sells the cups for $40 per box of 50 containers. Variable and fixed costs follow.

Manufacturing overhead$15,000

Total fixed costs$25,000

Suppose in September, the company produced and sold 3,000 boxes of beverage containers.

Required: Assuming the company is subject to a 40% income tax, determine the monthly unit sales required to produce a monthly after-tax profit of $4,500.

(a) Determine the unit contribution margin

Unit contribution margin = selling price per box − total variable cost per box.

ItemAmount
Unit contribution margin

(b) Gross the after-tax profit target up to pretax income

Required pretax income = desired after-tax profit ÷ (1 − tax rate).

ItemAmount
Required pretax income

(c) Solve for the required monthly unit sales

Required unit sales = (total fixed costs + required pretax income) ÷ unit contribution margin.
