Problem

French Broad Coffee Co.

Suppose French Broad Coffee Co. has a new shop in an Asheville, North Carolina shopping center that sells high-end teas and coffees. Further, suppose it has added smoothie drinks to its product line. Below are the assumed sales and cost data for the company.

CoffeeTeaSmoothie
Sales price per (12 oz.) serving$1.35$1.25$1.95
Variable cost per serving$0.60$0.45$0.75
Fixed costs per month$8,000

Assume that the company sells each month an average of 6,000 servings of coffee, 3,750 servings of tea, and 2,250 servings of smoothies.

Required: (a) Calculate French Broad Coffee Co.'s operating leverage ratio. (b) If sales increase by 20%, by how much will before-tax profit be expected to change? (c) If sales decrease by 20%, by how much will before-tax profit be expected to change?

(a) Operating leverage ratio

Operating leverage ratio = total contribution margin ÷ operating income (before-tax profit). Total contribution margin sums each product's unit contribution margin times its monthly servings.
