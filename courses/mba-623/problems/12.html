Cataloochee Supply Company

Cataloochee Supply Company of Waynesville analyzes its accounts receivable at December 31 and arrives at the age categories below, along with the percentages that are estimated as uncollectible. The balance of the allowance for uncollectible accounts is $1,100 on December 31, before any adjustments.

Aging of accountsAccounts receivableEstimated loss %
Required: (a) What amount of bad debts expense will Cataloochee report in its income statement for the year? (b) Use the financial statement effects template to record the bad debts expense for the year. (c) What is the balance of accounts receivable on its December 31 balance sheet?

Work in this order. The aging schedule gives you the allowance the balance sheet needs at year end. The expense is whatever it takes to move the allowance from the balance it already has to the balance it needs — the aging tells you the target, not the expense.
