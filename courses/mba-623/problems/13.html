Operating Expenses: Cost of Sales or SG&A?

Sorting a retailer’s operating costs into the two categories its income statement reports — and seeing where the line between getting goods to the shelf and everything else actually falls.

Problem

French Broad Stores Corporation

French Broad Stores Corporation, a general merchandise retailer headquartered in Asheville, discloses in its annual report the primary items classified in each major expense category: cost of sales (COS), or selling, general and administrative (SG&A).

For each expense below, indicate whether the item would be included in COS or SG&A.

Required: Classify all eighteen items.

One question resolves most of the list: does the cost move merchandise toward the point of sale, or does it run the rest of the business? Everything spent getting goods from a vendor into a store — freight, import duties, the distribution centers and the people in them, and anything that changes what the inventory is worth — sits in cost of sales. The stores themselves, the headquarters, and the cost of persuading and serving customers sit in SG&A.
