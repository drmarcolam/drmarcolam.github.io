Problem

Craggy Equipment Corp.

Following are selected accounts for Craggy Equipment Corp., a Hendersonville manufacturer of cranes and aerial work platforms, for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2027 ($ millions).

Required: (a) Indicate whether each account appears on the balance sheet (B) or the income statement (I). (b) Using the data below, compute total assets and total expenses.

(a) Classify each account

Choose B or I from the drop-down beside each account. Ask what the account measures: a balance at a point in time belongs on the balance sheet, an amount earned or incurred over the year belongs on the income statement.
