Problem

French Broad Farms

French Broad Farms, along the French Broad River near Asheville, North Carolina, grows organic vegetables and sells them to distributors and local restaurants after processing. The farm's leading product for restaurant customers is a mixture of organic green salad ingredients, prepared and ready to serve, sold by the bag.

Growing conditions have been very good this season and French Broad Farms has extra capacity. A representative of a restaurant association in another city has offered to buy fresh salad stock from the company to augment its regular supply during an upcoming festival.
