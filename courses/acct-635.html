ACCT 635

Governmental and Nonprofit Accounting

Understand how governments and nonprofit organizations account for and report on public resources, using fund accounting principles distinct from for-profit reporting.

Welcome to Your Course

In ACCT 635, you will learn the fund accounting principles governments and nonprofit organizations use to demonstrate accountability for public resources. Through real-world scenarios, you'll develop the skills to classify funds, record transactions, and prepare fund-level financial statements.
