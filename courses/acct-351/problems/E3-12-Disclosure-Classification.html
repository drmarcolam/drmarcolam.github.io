Exercise 3-12

Tuckasegee Outfitters, Incorporated

Tuckasegee Outfitters, Incorporated is preparing its annual report for the fiscal year ended December 31, —. The company’s controller has asked for your help in deciding how best to disclose information about the items below.

Required: Indicate whether each item should be disclosed (A), (B), or (C).
  1. In the summary of significant accounting policies note.
  2. In a separate disclosure note.
  3. On the face of the balance sheet itself.

Three questions, in order. Is it the method the company chose? That is the policies note. Does it appear as a line item or a parenthetical right on the statement? That is the face of the balance sheet. Everything else that needs explaining gets its own note.
