ACCT 605 · Advanced Financial Accounting · Chapter 10 · Operational Assets: Acquisition

The River District Store

What did the new Asheville store really cost? Classify the costs, capitalize the interest, and tell the CFO what it does to income and the covenant.

The situation

Blue Ridge Outfitters, Asheville, NC. Outdoor gear retailer with four stores. You are on the accounting staff; your reader is Dana Whitfield, CFO.

Blue Ridge is building a new flagship store in Asheville's River Arts District. The project is finished and the building is ready for use. Dana Whitfield needs to know what the building and land really cost, and what the project does to net income and the loan covenant. The accounting staff recorded all of the year's interest as expense and put some costs into the building that Luis Caldwell thinks do not belong there. Luis asks you for a memo to Dana.

Questions to answer

  1. Classify each cost in the table as Land, Land improvements, Building, or Expense, and give the cost of each asset. Which of the staff's entries are wrong?
  2. Compute the interest that should be capitalized. Show the weighted-average accumulated expenditures, the avoidable interest, and the comparison with actual interest. Say how the stoppage in your version affects the capitalization period.
  3. Prepare the cost of the new building, and the correcting entries needed to move from the staff's records to the correct amounts.
  4. What do the corrections do to net income and to liabilities divided by equity? Compare with the covenant limit.
  5. What should Dana understand about interest capitalization, so that she does not treat it as a way to improve earnings?

What to submit (two-page memo)

A memo of about two pages to Dana Whitfield, CFO, from the accounting staff (one file, Word or PDF). Use the headings To, From, Date, Re, then Summary, Analysis, Calculations (a table), and Recommendation.

  • Cite the authority you rely on next to each conclusion.
  • Put the interest calculation in a table with a header row.
  • Use heading styles, real tables, and plain language; add alt text to any picture.

Authority to start with

  • Textbook Chapter 10: costs included in property, plant and equipment, land and land improvements, and capitalized interest.
  • ASC 360-10-30-1 (cost of an asset includes costs to get it ready for use).
  • ASC 835-20-15, 835-20-25 and 835-20-30 (qualifying assets, the capitalization period, and avoidable interest). Look in particular at when capitalization continues through a stoppage and when it ends.

Check paragraph numbers against the FASB Codification yourself before you cite them.
