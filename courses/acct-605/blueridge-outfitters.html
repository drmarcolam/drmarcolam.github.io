ACCT 605 · Case

Blueridge Outfitters

Work as the accounting staff of an Asheville outdoor retailer. Each chapter brings a situation where the accounting is not obvious: you decide, support your answer with the textbook and the Codification, and show how it changes the statements.

Meet the company

Blue Ridge Outfitters is a family-owned outdoor gear retailer based in Asheville, North Carolina, with a storefront, an online marketplace and a wholesale line. The fiscal year ends December 31 and the company reports under U.S. GAAP. Inventory is perpetual FIFO and fixed assets are depreciated straight-line. The loan agreement with Mountain Credit Bank requires total liabilities divided by total equity to stay at or below 2.00. Ignore income taxes unless told otherwise.

Dana Whitfield — CFO: the covenant and steady growth
Luis Caldwell — Controller: getting it right and defensible
