Problem

French Broad Fabricators, Inc.

You have just been hired by French Broad Fabricators, Inc., a Marshall-based manufacturer of a revolutionary new garage door opening device. The president has asked you to review the company's costing system and help get better control of manufacturing overhead costs. The company has never used a flexible budget, and preparing one is your first step in overhead planning and control.

After analysis, you estimated the following cost formulas (based on machine-hours) and gathered the actual cost data below for March. During March, the company worked 14,000 machine-hours and produced 8,000 units. The company had originally planned to work 16,000 machine-hours during March.
