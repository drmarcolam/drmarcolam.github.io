Problem

Blue Ridge Regional Hospital

Blue Ridge Regional Hospital, located in Boone, uses a job-order costing system to assign costs to its patients. Direct materials (pharmaceutical drugs, heart valves, artificial hips, pacemakers, and the like) and direct labor (surgeons, anesthesiologists, radiologists, and nurses) associated with specific procedures are traced directly to individual patients. All other costs — equipment depreciation, insurance, utilities, incidental supplies, and around-the-clock monitoring labor — are treated as overhead and allocated based on the number of patient-days (each night a patient spends in the hospital).
