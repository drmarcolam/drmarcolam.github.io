Problem

The Ridgeline Tee Shop

Sam Ridgeline owns The Ridgeline Tee Shop, a shop in Cullowhee, NC that sells tee shirts and sweatshirts. Sam is considering a plan to hire students on commission to sell sweatshirts bearing the name and mascot of a local high school. Because the sweatshirts would carry a unique design, they must be ordered from the manufacturer six weeks in advance and cannot be returned. The manufacturer requires a minimum initial order of 129 sweatshirts, and any additional orders must also be in increments of 129 sweatshirts. Since no additional facilities would be needed to sell the sweatshirts, the only costs associated with the venture are the cost of the sweatshirts themselves and sales commissions.

Given data

ItemAmount
Cost per sweatshirt (from manufacturer)$12.00
Minimum / increment order quantity129 sweatshirts
Selling price per sweatshirt$19.50
Sales commission per sweatshirt$1.50

For requirements 1 and 3, treat both the sweatshirt cost and the commission as variable costs per unit sold — Sam can always place another order in increments of the minimum, so there is no fixed cost to the ongoing operation. For requirement 2, the initial order has already been placed and paid for and cannot be returned, so the cost of that initial order (minimum order quantity × cost per sweatshirt) becomes a committed, fixed cost for that batch — only the commission remains variable per shirt sold within it.

Required:
  1. Calculate the unit sales and dollar sales Sam needs to attain a target profit of $3,096.00. (Do not round intermediate calculations.)
  2. Assuming an initial order of 129 sweatshirts, calculate the break-even point in unit sales and dollar sales.
  3. How many sweatshirts would Sam need to sell to earn a target profit of $3,690.00?
