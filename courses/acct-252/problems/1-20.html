Direct and Indirect Costs; Variable and Fixed Costs — Haywood Seniors Care Center

Classifying the same set of costs along three dimensions at once: traceability to a program, traceability to the individuals served by it, and behavior with respect to volume.

Three cost objects, three questions per row: is the cost direct or indirect with respect to the Mobile Meals program as a whole? Is it direct or indirect with respect to a particular senior served by the program? And is it variable or fixed with respect to the number of seniors served? Each question is independent of the other two.
Problem

Haywood Seniors Care Center

Haywood Seniors Care Center, a Waynesville-based nonprofit, provides a variety of health services to the elderly. The center is organized into a number of departments, one of which is the Mobile Meals program that delivers hot meals to seniors in their homes on a daily basis.

Required: A number of costs of the center and the Mobile Meals program are listed below. For each cost, indicate whether it is a direct or indirect cost of the Mobile Meals program, whether it is a direct or indirect cost of particular seniors served by the program, and whether it is variable or fixed with respect to the number of seniors served.

Classify each cost

Ask three separate questions for each cost: could it be traced to the Mobile Meals program without allocation? Could it be traced to one particular senior without allocation? Does it rise and fall with the number of seniors served, or stay the same?

# Cost Direct/Indirect — Program Direct/Indirect — Seniors Variable/Fixed

A cost can be direct to the program yet indirect to any one senior — most Mobile Meals costs are traceable to the program but shared across everyone it serves, so they must be allocated to reach an individual.
