Problem

Nantahala Metal Works, Inc.

Nantahala Metal Works, Inc., a copper-fittings manufacturer near Bryson City, North Carolina, uses a standard cost system for its main product line. During the most recent month, the company purchased direct materials for $111,300, all of which were used in the production of 3,200 units. In addition, 4,900 direct labor-hours were worked on the product during the month, at a cost of $95,550.

Required:
  1. For direct materials:
    1. Compute the actual cost per foot of materials for the month.
    2. Compute the materials price variance and the materials spending (total) variance.
  2. For direct labor:
    1. Compute the standard direct labor rate per hour.
    2. Compute the standard hours allowed for the month's production.
    3. Compute the standard hours allowed per unit of product.
