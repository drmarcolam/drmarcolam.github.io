Problem

Blue Ridge Auto Trim Co.

Blue Ridge Auto Trim Co., based in Asheville, North Carolina, manufactures auto accessories including a set of seat covers that can be adjusted to fit most cars. According to its standards, the factory should work 1,035 hours each month to produce 2,070 sets of seat covers.

During the most recent month, the factory worked 500 direct labor-hours and produced 1,700 sets of covers. At standard, each set of covers should require 2.0 yards of material. All of the materials purchased during the month were used in production.
