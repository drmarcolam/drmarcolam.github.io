Assignment 1: Operating Governmental Funds

Record the General Fund and the Public Safety Grant Special Revenue Fund through the accounting cycle.

How this page works

Complete the journal entries, year-end adjustments and closing entries for both funds, then check your adjusted trial balances against the checkpoints. The Version 2 figures tie to Assignments 2 to 5.

  • Enter amounts in whole dollars, with no dollar signs. Pick each account from the list; the title must match the chart of accounts.
  • Use one line per account. Debit lines have an amount in Debit; credit lines have an amount in Credit.
  • Choose Check entry to score an entry. An entry earns 1 point when every line is right. Editing it later removes the point until you check it again.
  • Show model entry reveals the answer and the entry then earns no points.
  • Everyone works the same figures. Your work stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Finish

When you are done, choose Print / Save as PDF. Your name, Student ID, score and everything you entered print on the PDF. Upload that PDF to the Week 3 Deliverable in Canvas.
