Assignment 5: Government-Wide Conversion

Convert the reconciled fund results from Assignments 1 to 4 to the government-wide statements.

How this page works

Bring forward your fund results, reconcile fund balances and the change in fund balances to government-wide net position, trace the functional expenses, and complete the two government-wide statements. No correction entries are needed: Assignments 1 to 4 already tie.

  • Enter amounts in whole dollars, with no dollar signs. Enter decreases as negative numbers, for example -8500000 or (8500000).
  • Choose Check this part to score a part. Each correct cell earns 1 point. Editing a cell later removes its point until you check the part again.
  • Show answers reveals the answers for that part, and the part then earns no points.
  • The tie checks at the bottom update as you type. When every part is right, every tie holds.
  • Everyone works the same figures. Your work stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Tie checks

These update as you type. A correct set of answers ties everywhere.
