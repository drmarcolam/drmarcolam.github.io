ACCT 635 · Laurel Hollow · Week 3 Deliverable

Week 3 Deliverable: General Fund and Public Safety Grant Fund

Complete Llama Falls Assignments 1a and 1b, then upload the PDFs and your completion record to Canvas.

This deliverable practices these objectives

  1. Apply modified accrual: recognize revenues when measurable and available, and expenditures when the liability is incurred, including the main exceptions.
  2. Record the common General Fund revenue transactions (property tax levy, sales tax, fees) and the related receivables.
  3. Distinguish revenue from other financing sources, and record interfund transfers.
  4. Recognize revenue and expenditure on grants, including a cost-reimbursement grant.
  5. Classify restricted resources in a special revenue fund and explain fund balance categories.

How this deliverable works

The work for Week 3 is the Llama Falls case assignments below. 2 steps, 45 points in all. Your Student ID sets your scenario figures, so enter the same ID every time. Enter amounts in whole dollars and pick account titles from the dropdowns. Use Check my work as you go; the page updates its score.

Note. Use the same Student ID on 1a and 1b. The ID sets your scenario figures, and the two funds share a transfer.

Step 1 of 2 · 30 points

Assignment 1a: General Fund

Open Assignment 1a: General Fund (new tab)

On that page you will complete:

  • Transaction journal (T1 to T20, plus two budget amendments)
  • Year-end adjustments (A to G)
  • Closing entries (C1 to C3)
  • Trial balances, statements and budgetary comparison (they update as you enter)

When you finish, use the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of that page. Your name, Student ID and overall score print on the PDF.

Step 2 of 2 · 15 points

Assignment 1b: Public Safety Grant Special Revenue Fund

Open Assignment 1b: Public Safety Grant Special Revenue Fund (new tab)

On that page you will complete:

  • Transaction journal (SR-1 to SR-9)
  • Adjustments (H and I)
  • Closing entries
  • Trial balances and statements

When you finish, use the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of that page. Your name, Student ID and overall score print on the PDF.

Submit in Canvas

  1. Save the PDF from each assignment page (the Print / Save as PDF button on that page).
  2. Complete your completion record below and save it as a PDF too.
  3. Upload all the PDFs to the Week 3 Deliverable in Canvas.

Grading. Each step is worth the points shown. Your points for a step are the share of the page’s points that it shows as earned on your PDF, so a page showing 30 of 40 points earns 75% of that step’s value. Late or missing steps earn no points for that step.
