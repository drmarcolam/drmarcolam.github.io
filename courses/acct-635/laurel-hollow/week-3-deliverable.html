Week 3 Deliverable: General Fund and Public Safety Grant Fund

Complete Llama Falls Assignment 1 (Version 2), then upload the PDF and your completion record to Canvas.

This deliverable practices these objectives

  1. Apply modified accrual: recognize revenues when measurable and available, and expenditures when the liability is incurred, including the main exceptions.
  2. Record the common General Fund revenue transactions (property tax levy, sales tax, fees) and the related receivables.
  3. Distinguish revenue from other financing sources, and record interfund transfers.
  4. Recognize revenue and expenditure on grants, including a cost-reimbursement grant.
  5. Classify restricted resources in a special revenue fund and explain fund balance categories.

How this deliverable works

The work for Week 3 is the Llama Falls case assignments below. 2 steps, 45 points in all. Everyone works the same Version 2 figures, and the assignments tie to each other. Enter amounts in whole dollars and pick account titles from the dropdowns. Use Check entry as you go; the page updates its score.

Note. Both parts are on one page (Assignment 1). Print the page once, after you finish both parts. Your PDF shows a score for each fund.

Step 1 of 2 · 30 points

Assignment 1, Part 1: General Fund

Open Assignment 1, Part 1: General Fund (new tab)

On that page you will complete:

  • Transaction journal (GF-1 to GF-20)
  • Year-end adjustments (A1 to A7)
  • Closing entries (C1 to C4)
  • Adjusted trial balance and six checkpoints

When you finish, use the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of that page. Your name, Student ID, score and everything you entered print on the PDF.

Submit in Canvas

  1. Save a PDF from each assignment page (the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of that page).
  2. Complete your completion record below and save it as a PDF too.
  3. Upload all the PDFs to the Week 3 Deliverable in Canvas.

Grading. Each step is worth the points shown. Your points for a step are the share of that step’s points that your PDF shows as earned, so a fund showing 27 of 36 points earns 75% of that step’s value. A missing step earns no points.
