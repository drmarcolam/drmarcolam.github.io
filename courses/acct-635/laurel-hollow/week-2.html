Week 2 Walkthrough: Adopting the Budget

About 120 minutes. This is where the teaching happens. Enter your name and Student ID, read each step, write your answer to the checkpoint, then compare it with the model answer. At the end, save a PDF of your answers and the model answers and upload it to Canvas. Graded for completion.

0 of 5 compared
By the end of this walkthrough you will be able to:
  1. Explain the budget cycle and why the adopted budget is legal authority, including the North Carolina budget ordinance and which funds are budgeted through it.
  2. Compute a property tax levy and budgeted revenue, convert a rate per $100 to mills, and test how revenue responds to a change in the collection rate.
  3. Record the opening budgetary entry for a governmental fund, explain why Budgetary Fund Balance is zero in a balanced budget, and distinguish a budgetary interchange from a budgetary revision.
  4. Record an encumbrance through its life cycle and explain what an open encumbrance at year-end means.
  5. Read a budget-to-actual report and separate timing differences from real problems.
