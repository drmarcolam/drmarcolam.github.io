ReWelcome, and your first assignment

Welcome to Laurel Hollow. Our fiscal year began on July 1, and two new members join the council this month. At their orientation they asked a fair question: “Why does the city have so many separate sets of books?”

I need a one-page “fund map” that shows where each major activity lives, with a plain-language explanation of why. Start with the short slides and the walkthrough, then take the Gate Quiz. Your deliverable is due at the end of the block.

I also want you to meet one of our community partners. Pathways Together, a local nonprofit, serves families across the city, and we fund part of their work. You will hear more about them in the weeks ahead.

Welcome aboard.

By the end of this week you can

  1. Explain in plain language three reasons governments report differently from businesses: accountability to citizens, interperiod equity, and the budget as legal authority.
  2. Identify the main standard setters and describe how GAAP for governments is organized, including awareness that states such as North Carolina add their own requirements.
  3. Classify each of Laurel Hollow’s twelve activities by fund category and type, and state the measurement focus and basis of accounting for each category.
  4. Distinguish the government-wide statements from the fund statements at a preview level.

Time and flow

About 15 hours: intro slides, walkthrough and chapter summary, Gate Quiz, the fund map deliverable, and a short reflection. If you score below 80% on the Gate Quiz, choose one remedial path, then take the Mastery Quiz. The higher of the two quiz scores counts.

CPA relevance: fund classification, measurement focus and basis of accounting appear under State and Local Government Concepts in the FAR section (check the current AICPA blueprint).
