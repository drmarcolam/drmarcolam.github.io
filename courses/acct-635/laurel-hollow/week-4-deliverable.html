ACCT 635 · Laurel Hollow · Week 4 Deliverable

Week 4 Deliverable: Capital Projects and Debt Service Funds

Complete Llama Falls Assignment 2, then upload the PDF and your completion record to Canvas.

This deliverable practices these objectives

  1. Account for a capital project using a project ordinance, including the opening budgetary entry.
  2. Record the issuance of general obligation bonds, issuance costs, and transfers to the debt service fund.
  3. Record construction progress billings with retainage and encumbrance reversals.
  4. Record debt service in the debt service fund, with the correct timing for interest and principal.

How this deliverable works

The work for Week 4 is the Llama Falls case assignments below. 2 steps, 45 points in all. Your Student ID sets your scenario figures, so enter the same ID every time. Enter amounts in whole dollars and pick account titles from the dropdowns. Use Check my work as you go; the page updates its score.

Note. Both parts are on one page (Assignment 2). Use the same Student ID you used in Week 3.

Step 1 of 2 · 25 points

Assignment 2, Part 1: Capital Projects Fund (Community Recreation Center)

Open Assignment 2, Part 1: Capital Projects Fund (Community Recreation Center) (new tab)

On that page you will complete:

  • Transaction journal (CP-1 to CP-10)
  • Closing entry
  • Trial balances and statements

When you finish, use the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of that page. Your name, Student ID and overall score print on the PDF.

Step 2 of 2 · 20 points

Assignment 2, Part 2: Debt Service Fund

Open Assignment 2, Part 2: Debt Service Fund (new tab)

On that page you will complete:

  • Transaction journal (DS-1 to DS-8)
  • Adjustments (A and B)
  • Closing entry
  • Trial balances and statements

When you finish, use the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of that page. Your name, Student ID and overall score print on the PDF.

Submit in Canvas

  1. Save the PDF from each assignment page (the Print / Save as PDF button on that page).
  2. Complete your completion record below and save it as a PDF too.
  3. Upload all the PDFs to the Week 4 Deliverable in Canvas.

Grading. Each step is worth the points shown. Your points for a step are the share of the page’s points that it shows as earned on your PDF, so a page showing 30 of 40 points earns 75% of that step’s value. Late or missing steps earn no points for that step.
