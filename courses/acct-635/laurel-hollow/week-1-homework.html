B1 · Accountability and equity

A council member says, “The city should just aim to finish each year with a surplus.” In three sentences, explain why a government’s main reporting goal is accountability, and what interperiod equity asks of the year’s revenues.

Covers Gate Quiz 1, questions 1 and 2.

Solution

Governments do not have owners seeking a profit. They answer to citizens, the council, creditors and oversight bodies, who want to know whether public money was used as the law and the budget allowed. Interperiod equity asks whether this year’s revenues were enough to pay for this year’s services, so that today’s taxpayers do not pass the cost to future taxpayers. A surplus alone does not show either of these.

B2 · Rules and entity type

The Laurel Hollow Arts Council was incorporated under the nonprofit corporation act. It is funded by donations and ticket sales, its members elect its board, and it has no power to tax or to issue tax-exempt debt. (a) Is it governmental? (b) Which body sets its GAAP? (c) Which body sets GAAP for the City of Laurel Hollow?

Covers Gate Quiz 1, questions 3 and 11.

Solution

(a) No. It has none of the governmental characteristics: its board is not appointed by government officials, it has no taxing power, it cannot directly issue tax-exempt debt, and no government can dissolve it and take over its assets. Donations, fees and nonprofit incorporation do not make an entity governmental. (b) FASB. (c) GASB.

B3 · Measurement focus and basis

Name the three fund categories and the measurement focus and basis of each. In one sentence, say why governmental funds are measured differently from proprietary funds.

Covers Gate Quiz 1, question 9.

Solution

Governmental funds: current financial resources focus and modified accrual. Proprietary funds: economic resources focus and full accrual. Fiduciary funds: economic resources focus and full accrual. Governmental funds ask what resources are available to spend this year, while proprietary funds charge for services and need to show whether they cover all of their costs.

B4 · Two sets of statements

True or false, with a reason: the government-wide statements use modified accrual and include the officers’ 401(k) plan, which is a fiduciary fund.

Covers Gate Quiz 1, question 10.

Solution

False on both counts. Government-wide statements use the economic resources focus and full accrual. Fiduciary funds hold resources for others and are reported only in the fund statements.

B5 · Why separate funds

A resident asks, “Why not put everything in the General Fund?” Write a three-sentence reply.

Covers the purpose of funds (walkthrough steps 3 to 5).

Solution

Some money can be spent only for certain purposes because of law or agreements, so the city tracks it in its own fund to prove it followed the rules. Some activities, such as water service, run on their own fees and should show whether they cover their costs. Separate funds let the council and the public see each one clearly.
