Week 1 Walkthrough: Why Government Accounting Is Different, and Where the Money Is Kept

About 90 minutes. This is where the teaching happens. Read each step, write your answer to the checkpoint, then compare it with the model answer. At the end, copy your answers into the Canvas submission box. Graded for completion.

0 of 5 compared
By the end of this walkthrough you will be able to:
  1. Explain in plain language three reasons governments report differently from businesses: accountability to citizens, interperiod equity, and the budget as legal authority.
  2. Identify the main standard setters and describe how GAAP for governments is organized, including the North Carolina overlay.
  3. Classify each of Laurel Hollow's twelve activities by fund category and type, and state the measurement focus and basis of accounting for each category.
  4. Distinguish the government-wide statements from the fund statements at a preview level.
