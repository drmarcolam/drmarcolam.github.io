How the tutor works.
  1. Pick a chapter and a type of problem below. Every problem is new, with new names and numbers.
  2. You answer one step at a time. Type a number or choose an answer, then select Check my answer.
  3. If you are right, you get praise and the next step.
  4. If you are not, you get a friendly hint and one more try. If you miss it again, you will see the solution for that step and then move on to the next one.
Round as each step says. Enter a negative amount with a minus sign or in parentheses, for example -4,000 or (4,000).

Practice problems are modeled on the ACCT 635 chapter notes, chapter walkthroughs, and textbook problems. All names and numbers are fictional and are generated at random each time, so you can try as many problems as you like. · ACCT 635 · Western Carolina University
