Background

Town of Waynesville — Employees' Retirement Fund

The Town of Waynesville, North Carolina, has had an employee pension fund for several years. The following is a trial balance for the fund at December 31, 2025, and several transactions that occurred during 2026. Prepare (a) the journal entries necessary to record the 2026 transactions, (b) a statement of changes in fiduciary net position for the fund for 2026, and (c) a statement of fiduciary net position as of December 31, 2026.
