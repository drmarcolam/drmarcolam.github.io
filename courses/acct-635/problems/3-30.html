Background

Jackson County

The Jackson County, North Carolina, board of commissioners approved the budget for fiscal 2026. Revenues from property taxes are budgeted at $800,000. According to the county tax assessor, the assessed valuation of all property in the county is $50,000,000. Of this amount, property worth $10,000,000 belongs to the federal government or to religious organizations and, therefore, is not subject to property taxes. In addition, certificates for the following exemptions have been filed:

In the past, uncollectible property taxes have averaged about 3% of the levy. This rate is not expected to change in the foreseeable future. Using all of this information, determine the items below.
