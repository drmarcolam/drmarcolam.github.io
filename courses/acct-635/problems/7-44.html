Journal Entries and Financial Statements — Black Mountain Metro Parking Fund

Recording the construction financing and first two years of operations of an Enterprise Fund parking facility, then preparing its statement of revenues, expenses, and changes in net position and its statement of net position.

Background

Black Mountain Metro Parking Fund

The Metro Central Railroad is a commuter railroad that stops at the Town of Black Mountain, North Carolina. Black Mountain, a growing community, decides to construct and operate a parking lot near the railroad station to accommodate the needs of its citizens. The activities of the parking lot will be accounted for in an Enterprise Fund, known as the Black Mountain Metro Parking Fund, because the activity will be financed with debt secured solely by pledge of the facility's net revenues from parking fees. This problem covers transactions and events during calendar years 2026 and 2027.

Part (a) — Transactions and events during 2026

Item 1

Revenue bonds sold at par

The Fund receives $5,000,000 from the sale of revenue bonds at par. The revenue bonds were sold on July 1, 2026. They mature at the rate of $250,000 a year over a period of 20 years, starting July 1, 2027. Interest on the bonds is payable annually, also starting July 1, 2027, at 4 percent per annum on the outstanding debt.
