Special Revenue Fund Accounting — Scotts Creek Park

Recording a year of activity in the Town of Sylva's Scotts Creek Park Special Revenue Fund — budget adoption, revenue collection, encumbrances, a budget amendment, and year-end accruals — then preparing the fund's financial statements and its budgetary comparison schedule.

Leave the ID blank and click to restore the original textbook figures.
Before you start: the Scotts Creek Park Special Revenue Fund began the year with a $0 fund balance, and the fund balance is classified entirely as Committed. Enter all dollar amounts in whole dollars (e.g., 285,000). In the journal-entry tables, use the account dropdown and put each amount in the correct Debit or Credit column — there is no Dr/Cr label to guide you. Negative amounts (e.g., an unfavorable variance) should be entered with a minus sign, e.g., -4,000.
