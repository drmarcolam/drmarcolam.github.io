Sales taxes collected by the state

Sales taxes are collected by the state on behalf of the county. On January 20, 2027, the state advised the county that (a) it would send the county a check for $150,000 before February 29, 2027, for taxes collected by merchants for the fourth quarter of 2026; and (b) based on past experience, it anticipated sending an additional check for approximately $45,000 during March or April for taxes received from late filers. Item 3

State reimbursement grant

Pursuant to state law, the county receives an annual grant from the state to inspect nursing homes and day care centers within the county. The law provides that the state will reimburse the county for 50 percent of salary and travel costs incurred by the county, but no more than the amount appropriated in the state budget for that purpose. For 2026 the amount appropriated by the state for Watauga County was $240,000. Watauga received an advance payment of $175,000 in January 2026 and was to apply for any additional amount due to the county before January 15, 2027. Watauga determined that it had spent a total of $520,000 (covering both the county's share and the state's share of the costs) on the program. (Assume Watauga previously recorded the $175,000 advance as an Advance from State Government, and the $520,000 of expenditures.)
