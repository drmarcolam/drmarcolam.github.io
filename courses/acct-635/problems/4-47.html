Complete Accounting Cycle — City of Hendersonville General Fund

Recording a full year of General Fund activity for the City of Hendersonville, North Carolina — budget adoption, a tax anticipation note, the property tax levy, encumbrances, a mid-year budget interchange, and year-end accruals — then preparing the preclosing trial balance, the fund's financial statements and budgetary comparison schedule, and the closing entries.

In the journal-entry tables, use the account dropdown and put each amount in the correct Debit or Credit column — there is no Dr/Cr label to guide you. In the preclosing trial balance, put each account's balance in the correct Debit or Credit column as well.
