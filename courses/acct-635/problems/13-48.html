Background

Highlands Fine Arts Center (HFAC)

Highlands Fine Arts Center (HFAC) is a museum in Highlands, North Carolina, dedicated to collecting and displaying works by North Carolina artists. It acquires works of art primarily from direct donations and from income provided by permanent endowments. It does not, however, capitalize its art collection, and does not recognize revenue when art works are contributed to the collection. HFAC protects and cares for its art works and has a policy of using the proceeds from the sale of collection items solely to acquire new works of art and to provide direct care for collection items. HFAC charges all expenses to four programs: curatorial and exhibition, art acquisition, education and public service, and administration.
