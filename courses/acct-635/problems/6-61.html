Lease Accounting — Town of Waynesville

Preparing the general journal entries needed to record a noncancellable equipment lease in the General Fund: the entry at the commencement of the lease, the first lease payment (made the same day the lease is signed), and the second lease payment a year later, once interest has begun to accrue.

Before you start: record every entry in the General Fund's general journal, using control accounts only (no subsidiary-ledger detail is required). Enter all dollar amounts in whole dollars (e.g., 75,152), and use the account dropdown to select the exact account title for each line. The Town disburses cash through a voucher system, so each lease payment is recorded in two steps: first the voucher (an obligation to pay), then the payment of that voucher in cash.
Background

Town of Waynesville

On January 1, 2026, the finance director of the Town of Waynesville, North Carolina, signed a noncancellable lease for street maintenance equipment. The lease is for 10 years. The present value of the payments expected to be made during the lease term is $75,152. The Town's incremental borrowing rate is 7 percent. The annual lease payment of $10,000 is due on the first day of each year, beginning on the day the lease was signed.

Prepare the journal entries necessary to record the lease transaction for 2026 and the lease payment made in 2027. Assume the Town uses a voucher system.

Item 1

Commencement of the lease — January 1, 2026

At the commencement of the lease, the General Fund records the lease as a financed acquisition of a capital asset: an expenditure equal to the present value of the payments expected to be made, offset by an other financing source.

Required: Prepare the journal entry to record the lease at its commencement.

Item 2

First lease payment — January 1, 2026

The first lease payment of $10,000 is due the same day the lease is signed, so no time has passed for interest to accrue. The entire payment reduces the lease obligation and is recorded as a debt service—principal expenditure.

Required: Prepare the journal entries to (a) record the voucher for the first lease payment and (b) record payment of that voucher.

Item 3

Second lease payment — January 1, 2027

One year has now passed on the remaining lease obligation, so the second payment of $10,000 must be split between interest (at the Town's incremental borrowing rate, applied to the balance outstanding after the first payment) and principal.

Required: Prepare the journal entries to (a) record the voucher for the second lease payment, separating interest from principal, and (b) record payment of that voucher.
