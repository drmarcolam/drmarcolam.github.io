Journal Entries, Statement of Activities, and Statement of Functional Expenses — Sing Sing Singers

Preparing the journal entries to record a year's activity for a nonprofit opera company based in Jackson County, North Carolina, and then rolling that activity forward into a statement of activities and a statement of functional expenses.

Background

Sing Sing Singers (SSS)

Sing Sing Singers (SSS) is a local opera company based in Sylva, Jackson County, North Carolina. As a nonprofit, it finances its activities through contributions and admissions fees. It accounts for its expenses through three major functions: regular events, special events, and administration. For each function, it maintains three accounts by nature of expense: salaries, supplies and other, and occupancy.

On July 1, the start of its fiscal year 2025, SSS had cash on hand amounting to $12,000, of which $7,000 represented net assets without donor restrictions and $5,000 represented net assets with donor restrictions. The following transactions and events occurred during the year. Prepare journal entries to record these transactions; also prepare a statement of activities and a statement of expenses by function and nature for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026.

Item 1

Restriction satisfied by passage of time

The $5,000 of net assets with donor restrictions resulted from a donation made during the previous fiscal year that could be used only during the current fiscal year. On July 1 of the current fiscal year, the net assets became available for spending.

Required: Prepare the journal entry to reclassify the net assets.

Item 9

Donated services

SSS received the benefit of the following donated services during the year:

  • (a) A local attorney prepared contracts with the performers. Had the attorney charged for this work, the cost to SSS would have been $4,000. Charge this to the administration function.
  • (b) A local carpenter constructed sets for the regular events. Had the carpenter charged for this work, the cost to SSS would have been $8,000. Charge this to the regular events function, supplies and other.
  • (c) Two local bankers served as ushers for all performances. Had SSS paid for this work, the cost would have been $1,000.
Required: Prepare the journal entries needed to record these donated services, if any.

The attorney's and carpenter's services meet that test; the ushers' service does not, so no entry is made for item (c).
