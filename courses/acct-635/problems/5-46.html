Preparing the general journal entries to record a year's worth of property tax activity for a small North Carolina municipality: the tax levy, collections, recognition of previously deferred revenue, tax lien foreclosure and sale, a taxpayer rebate, and year-end reclassification of delinquent and unavailable taxes.
