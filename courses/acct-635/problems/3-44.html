Budgetary Transactions and Events — Town of Sylva

Preparing the general journal entries — including the budgetary entries — to record a year's worth of General Fund activity for a small North Carolina municipality: budget adoption, revenue collection, expenditures, a mid-year budget amendment, and purchase-order encumbrances.

Before you start: record every entry in the General Fund's general journal, using budgetary and control accounts only (no subsidiary-ledger detail is required except where an entry names a specific department, as in item 4). Enter all dollar amounts in whole dollars (e.g., 285,000), and use the account dropdown to select the exact account title for each line. Item 4

Invoice shortfall and budget amendment

The administrator of fringe benefits received an invoice for police employee health insurance in the amount of $95,000, together with a letter from the insurance provider explaining that the large increase was caused by a change in federal law. The invoice could not be paid because the Police—fringe benefits appropriation had a balance of only $60,000 (appropriation of $450,000 minus expenditures in item 3 of $390,000). As a result, the town commissioners amended the budget as follows:

Budget amendmentAmount
Increase: appropriation for Police—fringe benefits$35,000
Decrease: appropriation for Police—salaries$5,000
Decrease: appropriation for Police—supplies$25,000
Use of fund balance$5,000
Required: Prepare the journal entry to record the budget amendment.
