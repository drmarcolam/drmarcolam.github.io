Background

City of Brevard — College Scholarship Private-Purpose Trust Fund

Prepare (a) the journal entries for 2026 necessary to record the following events and transactions for the College Scholarship Private-Purpose Trust Fund, a fiduciary component unit of the City of Brevard, North Carolina; (b) a statement of changes in fiduciary net position for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026, for the fund; (c) a statement of fiduciary net position as of that date; and (d) the 2026 closing entry (entries) for the fund.

Item 1

The gift that establishes the trust

A prominent citizen funds the trust for the College Scholarship Fund with $2,000,000 from her estate, based on the provisions of her will. The City of Brevard controls the trust assets. The terms of the will require the trust to maintain the principal intact; investment income is to be used to provide college scholarships for deserving graduates of Brevard High School, which is independent from the City of Brevard.
