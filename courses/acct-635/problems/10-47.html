Modified Approach for Infrastructure Asset Reporting — City of Hendersonville

The city of Hendersonville adopted the modified approach for reporting its roads and bridges. The city met the GAAP requirements for having an asset management system, making periodic condition assessments, and estimating the annual amount needed to preserve the roads and bridges. Based on the data below, (1) prepare journal entries to record the transactions in the city's governmental funds, and (2) prepare the adjusting entries needed to report roads and bridges in the city's government-wide statements for the year ending December 31, 2026. All amounts are in thousands.

Item a

Adoption of the modified approach

At December 31, 2025, the city's infrastructure assets (roads and bridges) had an acquisition value of $11,600. Because the city retroactively adopted the modified approach, no accumulated depreciation is reported for these assets, and no expenditure or expense is recognized for maintaining the physical condition of the network at or above the established condition level.

Item b

Preservation expenditures

During 2026, the city incurred $1,845 of preservation expenditures on its roads and bridges, paid in cash. Preservation costs that only maintain the infrastructure network at (rather than above) the condition level the city has established are expensed as incurred under the modified approach—at both the fund level and the government-wide level—rather than capitalized.

Required: Prepare (1) the fund-level journal entry to record the cash payment and (2) the government-wide adjusting/conversion entry needed to convert the fund-level expenditure into a government-wide expense.

Item c

Capacity-adding improvement

During 2026, the city paid $725 to contractors to add two additional lanes to its 49th Street bridge. Unlike ordinary preservation costs, an addition that increases the capacity or efficiency of an infrastructure asset is capitalized—even under the modified approach—because it is not merely maintaining the network at its established condition level.

Required: Prepare (1) the fund-level journal entry to record the cash payment and (2) the government-wide adjusting/conversion entry needed to capitalize the addition as an infrastructure asset.
