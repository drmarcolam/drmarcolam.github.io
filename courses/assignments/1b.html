Public Safety Grant Special Revenue Fund

A state public-safety grant reimburses the City for 80% of qualifying program expenditures, subject to a maximum state contribution. This fund does not use formal budgetary integration (no Estimated Revenues/Appropriations accounts) — only encumbrance control.

Background

Special Revenue Fund — beginning balances

The Special Revenue Fund has no beginning balances (it was newly established this fiscal year). State reimbursement rate: 80% of qualifying expenditures, subject to a maximum state contribution of $800,000 for the grant period.
