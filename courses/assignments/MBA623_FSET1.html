Challenge 1 — From Transactions to Statements

Blue Ridge Outdoor Gear Inc. designs and sells hiking equipment, trail accessories, and annual membership services. Record a month's worth of transactions on the financial statement effects template (FSET), then summarize the results.

Recommended after Module 3 · 25 points · Expected time 25–30 minutes

Background

Blue Ridge Outdoor Gear Inc.

Blue Ridge Outdoor Gear Inc. designs and sells hiking equipment, trail accessories, and annual membership services. The company sells through its website and regional retailers. It is growing quickly, and management wants a clean record of how each of the following events affects the accounting equation.

Events

Transactions

  1. Owners invest cash in exchange for common stock.
  2. The company purchases inventory for cash.
  3. It sells inventory that cost for cash.
  4. It pays cash for a 12-month insurance policy beginning October 1.
  5. It receives cash on December 1 for six months of TrailPass service beginning that day.
  6. At December 31, employees have earned that will be paid in January.
  7. Record the December 31 insurance adjustment and the TrailPass revenue earned through December 31.
Required:
  1. Complete the FSET for all events (nine rows total — items 3 and 7 each split into two rows).
  2. Compute ending cash, inventory, prepaid insurance, deferred revenue, wages payable, contributed capital, and earned capital.
  3. Prepare a short income statement and classify each cash flow as operating, investing, or financing.
  4. In 75 words or fewer, explain why cash received does not always equal revenue.
