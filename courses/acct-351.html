Welcome to Your Course

In ACCT 351, you will study the conceptual framework of financial accounting and the process of preparing financial statements under U.S. GAAP. You will work through the accounting for cash, receivables, inventory, long-lived assets, and related topics, developing the skill to analyze transactions, apply authoritative standards, and report results accurately.
