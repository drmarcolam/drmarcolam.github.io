Part b

Character of the gain or loss

The building is nonresidential real property depreciated straight-line, so there is no §1250 ordinary recapture; the gain attributable to depreciation is “unrecaptured §1250 gain,” which is part of the §1231 gain but is taxed at a maximum of 25%. The equipment is §1245 property: any gain is ordinary income to the extent of the depreciation taken, and only the excess is §1231 gain. A loss on either asset is a §1231 loss. Assume Lily has no nonrecaptured net §1231 losses from the five preceding years.
