Problem

Blackrock Brewing Company, Inc.

Blackrock Brewing Company, Inc., a craft brewery headquartered in Asheville, North Carolina, reports the following transactions relating to its stock accounts during fiscal 2027. Record each transaction using the financial statement effects template.

    Blackrock had no treasury shares outstanding before March 31, and every treasury share purchased on March 31 is resold by July 15.

    Required: Record each transaction in the template below. For every column a transaction affects, enter the amount on the first line and choose the account title on the second.

    These are all transactions with the company's own owners, so none of them generate revenue or expense — the Revenue, Expenses, and Net Income columns should stay empty on every row, and so should Noncash Assets and Liabilities. That leaves Cash and Contributed Capital to carry every dollar, even on July 15, when the cash received is less than what those shares originally cost. A shortfall like that still reduces Contributed Capital directly; it is never run through the income statement.
