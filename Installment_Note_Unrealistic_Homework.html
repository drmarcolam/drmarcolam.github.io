Notes with Unrealistic Interest Rates

Recording a note whose stated rate doesn't reflect market terms at its present value, then comparing an interest-only note against an equal-payment installment note built from the same cash price.

Notes Payable

A&G Warehousers (Buyer)

Ionescu Technologies, Incorporated, constructed a conveyor for A&G Warehousers that was completed and ready for use on January 1, 2027. A&G paid for the conveyor by issuing a $100,000, four-year note that specified 4% interest to be paid on December 31 of each year, with the note to be repaid at the end of four years. The conveyor was custom-built, so its cash price was unknown; by comparison with similar transactions, a reasonable interest rate was determined to be 9%.

Face amount of the note$100,000

Term4 years
