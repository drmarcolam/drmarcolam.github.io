Comprehensive Problem

Pisgah Steel Corporation

On December 31, 2026, Pisgah Steel Corporation had 630,000 shares of common stock and 303,000 shares of 7%, noncumulative, nonconvertible preferred stock issued and outstanding.

Pisgah issued a 4% common stock dividend on May 15 and paid cash dividends of $430,000 and $72,000 to common and preferred shareholders, respectively, on December 15, 2027.

On February 28, 2027, Pisgah sold 54,000 common shares. In keeping with its long-term share repurchase plan, 2,000 shares were retired on July 1.

Pisgah's net income for the year ended December 31, 2027, was $2,250,000. The income tax rate is 25%.

Also, as part of a 2026 agreement for the acquisition of Nantahala Cable Company, another 27,000 shares (already adjusted for the stock dividend) are to be issued to former Nantahala shareholders on December 31, 2028, if Nantahala's 2028 net income is at least $530,000. In 2027, Nantahala's net income was $660,000.

As part of an incentive compensation plan, Pisgah granted incentive stock options to division managers on December 31 of the current and each of the previous two years. Each option permits its holder to buy one share of common stock at an exercise price equal to market value at the date of grant and can be exercised one year from that date.
