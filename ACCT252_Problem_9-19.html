Flexible budget performance report

Planning budget = cost formula applied at the originally planned pizzas/deliveries. Flexible budget = cost formula applied at the actual pizzas/deliveries. Activity variance = flexible budget − planning budget. Revenue and spending variance = actual results − flexible budget.
