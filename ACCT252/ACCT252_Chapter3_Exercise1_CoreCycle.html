Recall card: Chapter 3 formulas
  • Estimated total overhead (Y = a + bX): a = fixed overhead, b = variable overhead rate per unit of the base, X = estimated amount of the base.
  • Predetermined overhead rate = Estimated total overhead ÷ Estimated total amount of the allocation base.
  • Overhead applied to a job = Predetermined rate × Actual amount of the base used by the job.
  • Total job cost = Direct materials + Direct labor + Overhead applied.
  • Unit product cost = Total job cost ÷ Units produced.

Need more review? Check your Chapter 3 class notes or the Chapter 3 section of your textbook.

Part 1

Which Formula?

Before any numbers, decide which calculation fits each question. If you are unsure, use the recall card above.

Check 1 — Choose the Calculation
Need a hint?
The rate is set before the year starts. Think about what is known at that point and what is only known later.
Solution

The rate is estimated overhead ÷ estimated base, because it must be set before the year starts. Overhead is applied to a job with that rate × the hours (or other base) the job actually used. Unit product cost is the total job cost, including applied overhead, ÷ the units produced.

Wrap-up

The Core Cycle at a Glance

  • Estimate total overhead with Y = a + bX, then rate = estimated overhead ÷ estimated base.
  • Applied = rate × the actual base the job used.
  • Job cost = direct materials + direct labor + applied overhead; unit cost = job cost ÷ units.
