Planning vs flexible

Planning Budgets and Flexible Budgets

Highlands Catering in Highlands, North Carolina, budgeted for 2,000 guests this season (the planning budget) but actually served 2,200 guests. Cost formulas:

Revenue and cost formulas

Revenue$50 per guest
Ingredients$12 per guest
Wages$15,000 + $5 per guest
Utilities$4,000 + $1 per guest
Rent$8,000 fixed
A planning budget is prepared for one planned level of activity and is not adjusted. A flexible budget is adjusted to the actual level of activity, using the cost formulas (Y = a + bX).

Activity variances

Activity Variances

Activity variance = Flexible budget amount − Planning budget amount. It arises solely because the actual level of activity differed from the plan. It says nothing about how well costs were controlled.
Example 1 — Revenue Activity Variance

What is the revenue activity variance?

$
Solution

Flexible $110,000 − planning $100,000 = $10,000. Favorable: more guests than planned means more revenue.

Revenue activity variance = $10,000 F

Example 2 — Ingredients Activity Variance

What is the activity variance for ingredients?

$
Solution

Flexible $26,400 − planning $24,000 = $2,400. Unfavorable in the sense that costs are higher, but this is only because more guests were served.

Ingredients activity variance = $2,400 U

Example 3 — Favorable or Unfavorable?

For each item, is the activity variance favorable (F) or unfavorable (U)? Recall that higher-than-planned activity increases both revenue and variable costs.

Solution

Revenue is higher than planned (F). Variable ingredients cost is higher than planned (U). Rent is fixed, so it is the same in the planning and flexible budgets and has no activity variance.

Solution

  • Revenue variance = Actual revenue − Flexible budget revenue (higher is favorable).
  • Spending variance = Actual cost − Flexible budget cost (higher is unfavorable).
Both compare actual results with the flexible budget, so they remove the effect of activity.
Example 1 — Revenue Variance

Wrap-up

Chapter 9 at a Glance

  • Planning budget = planned activity; flexible budget = actual activity.
  • Activity variance = flexible − planning (caused only by activity differing from plan).
  • Revenue and spending variances = actual vs. flexible budget; they measure how well revenues and costs were managed.
  • The flexible budget goes between the planning budget and actual results in a performance report.
