Depletion and Change in Estimate

Computing depletion of a mineral mine and units-of-production depreciation on related structures and equipment, then applying a change in estimate to the remaining recoverable tons.

Rounding convention: round per-ton rates to 4 decimal places; round all final dollar figures to the nearest whole dollar.

Problem 1 of 1

Cullowhee Mining Company

In 2027, Cullowhee Mining Company purchased land containing a mineral mine for $1,750,000. Additional costs of $672,000 were incurred to develop the mine. Geologists estimated that 430,000 tons of ore would be extracted. After the ore is removed, the land will have a resale value of $100,000.

To aid extraction, Cullowhee built structures and small storage buildings at the site at a cost of $167,700. These structures have a useful life of 10 years but cannot be moved after the ore is removed and will be left at the site. New equipment costing $98,600 was also purchased and installed; it will not be moved to another site, but can be sold at auction for $4,000 after the mining project is completed.

In 2027, 53,000 tons of ore were extracted and sold. In 2028, the estimate of total tons of ore in the mine was revised from 430,000 to 517,500. During 2028, 83,000 tons were extracted, of which 63,000 tons were sold.

Cullowhee uses the units-of-production method to determine depreciation on mining facilities and equipment, tied to the same estimated tons used for depletion.

Requirement 1. Compute depletion and depreciation of the mine and the mining facilities and equipment for 2027 and 2028.

2027 (Original Estimate: 430,000 Tons)

ItemAmount
Mine: depletion rate ($1,750,000 + $672,000 − $100,000 residual, ÷ 430,000 tons)
Structures: depreciation rate ($167,700 ÷ 430,000 tons)
Equipment: depreciation rate ($98,600 − $4,000 residual, ÷ 430,000 tons)
Mine: 2027 depletion (53,000 tons extracted)
Structures: 2027 depreciation
Equipment: 2027 depreciation
Per-ton rates are checked to 4 decimal places.

2028 (Revised Estimate: 464,500 Tons Remaining)

The revised total (517,500 tons) minus the 53,000 tons already extracted leaves 464,500 recoverable tons as of the start of 2028. Apply each asset's remaining book value over this new remaining-tons estimate (a change in accounting estimate, applied prospectively).

ItemAmount
Mine: book value, start of 2028 (depletable base − 2027 depletion)
Structures: book value, start of 2028
Equipment: book value, start of 2028
Mine: new depletion rate (book value ÷ 464,500 remaining tons)
Structures: new depreciation rate
Equipment: new depreciation rate
Mine: 2028 depletion (83,000 tons extracted)
Structures: 2028 depreciation
Equipment: 2028 depreciation
Per-ton rates are checked to 4 decimal places; dollar figures allow a small rounding tolerance.
