Information for the adjustments

    Required: Record each adjustment in the template below. For every column an adjustment affects, enter the amount on the first line and choose the account title on the second.

    One observation is worth making before you start: every one of these adjustments was prompted by the calendar, not by a payment or a receipt. The cash for the insurance, the supplies and the rent has already moved, and the salaries will be paid in February. If you find yourself entering an amount in the cash column, look again.
