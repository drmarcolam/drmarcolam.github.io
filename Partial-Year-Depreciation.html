Problem 1 of 1

Valle Crucis Toy Company

On April 1, 2025, Valle Crucis Toy Company purchased equipment for use in its manufacturing process. The equipment cost $51,200, has a 10-year useful life, and has no residual value. The company uses straight-line depreciation for all manufacturing equipment.

On January 4, 2027, $12,950 was spent to repair the equipment and add a feature that increased its operating efficiency. Of the total, $2,300 represented ordinary repairs and annual maintenance and $10,650 represented the cost of the new feature. In addition to improving efficiency, the total useful life of the equipment was extended to 12 years.

Any capitalized amount is recorded using Alternative 2 — capitalization of new cost (the improvement cost is added to the asset's book value).

Preliminary step. Compute the original annual depreciation, book value at the improvement date, the new book value after capitalizing the improvement, and the new annual depreciation rate.
