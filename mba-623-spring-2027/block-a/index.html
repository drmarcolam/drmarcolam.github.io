Block A: Reading and Recording Cullowhee’s Statements

Modules 1–3 · Cullowhee Cycle Works

How each module works. (1) Complete the interactive walkthrough (required for everyone). (2) Take the Gate Quiz in Canvas (10 questions, one attempt, closed-AI). 8 of 10 (80%) meets the gate. (3) At 80% or higher you are finished with the module, and the Mastery Quiz is optional. Below 80%, Canvas assigns your Step 3 path: the Remedial Chapter Walkthrough, the lecture path, or practice problems, plus the Mastery Quiz. The higher quiz score counts. The quizzes are in Canvas; always follow the instructions there for what to submit.
