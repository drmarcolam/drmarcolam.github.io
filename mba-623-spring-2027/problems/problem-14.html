How to answer. Classify every line as operating (it arises from running the business), nonoperating (it arises from how the company invests spare cash or finances itself), or neither for income tax, which is levied on the combined result. Enter the subtotals in whole thousands; enter a net expense as a negative number. For any gain or loss, ask which asset produced it: one earned on an asset the company uses to make and sell its products belongs with operations, wherever the statement prints it.
