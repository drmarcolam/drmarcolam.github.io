(d) Explaining the difference

Which statement best explains why the ratios of the two companies differ?

  1. Ridgeline is much larger than Valley Value, so its ratios are naturally larger.
  2. Valley Value is a younger company, so its ratios are naturally lower.
  3. Valley Value imports its bikes from Asia, which keeps its product costs down.
  4. Ridgeline’s brand recognition gives it pricing power: it can add a bigger markup to what it sells, so more of each sales dollar is left after the cost of sales.
Explanation. Ridgeline keeps about 47.0 cents of every sales dollar as gross profit against about 25.0 cents for Valley Value. The premium brand commands a higher price over cost. Valley Value makes up part of the gap in returns through volume.
