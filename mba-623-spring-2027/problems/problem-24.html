Scenario. Ridgeline Bikes, Inc. reports the following transactions relating to its stock accounts during fiscal 2027. Record the effects of each transaction. Ridgeline had no treasury shares before March 31.
  1. Feb. 3 — Issued 30,000 shares of $5 par value common stock at $26 cash per share.
  2. Feb. 27 — Issued 6,000 shares of $50 par value, 8% preferred stock at $84 cash per share.
  3. Mar. 31 — Purchased 4,000 shares of its own common stock (treasury stock) at $28 cash per share.
  4. June 25 — Resold 2,500 shares of its treasury stock at $40 cash per share.
  5. July 15 — Resold 1,000 shares of its treasury stock at $24 cash per share.
How to answer. Enter whole dollars with no cents. Each transaction has up to three effects; use as many lines as you need and leave the rest empty (empty lines are not scored, but anything entered on a line that should be empty is marked wrong). For each effect pick the element and account, whether it increases or decreases, and the amount. Direction is the effect on the element: buying treasury stock decreases contributed capital, and reselling treasury shares increases it. Any amount above or below the treasury stock’s cost is recorded in additional paid-in capital.
Required. Record each transaction, then compute the net effect on total contributed capital for the year and the Treasury Stock account balance at year end.
