Skyline funds itself with a lower proportion of equity, and a higher proportion of debt, than Plainfield. How does that affect the evaluation of their relative riskiness?

  1. Plainfield is riskier because a greater share of its assets is current rather than long-term.
  2. Skyline carries the greater financial risk: a larger share of its assets is funded by creditors, whose interest and principal must be paid on schedule whatever the year brings, while Plainfield’s heavier reliance on equity leaves it more room to absorb a bad year.
  3. Skyline is riskier simply because it is the larger company.
  4. Capital structure has no bearing on riskiness, since both balance sheets balance by construction.
Explanation. Liabilities fund 68.48% of Skyline’s assets but only 27.14% of Plainfield’s.
