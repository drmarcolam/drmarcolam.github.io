Explanation. Every customer covers its traceable costs in part (a), but Customers B, C and E earn thin margins, and after allocating $1,832,000 of remaining G&A by sales dollars, B, C and E show losses. Allocated overhead is not avoidable if a customer leaves, so part (a) is the better guide to which customers add to profit; part (b) shows whether the total relationship earns its full share of overhead.
