Problem

Swannanoa Cleaning Co.

J. Buckner started Swannanoa Cleaning Co., a cleaning services firm in Swannanoa, on April 1. The company created the following accounts to record the transactions for April: Cash; Accounts receivable; Supplies; Prepaid van lease; Equipment; Notes payable; Accounts payable; Common stock; Dividends; Cleaning fees revenue; Wages expense; Advertising expense; and Fuel expense.

Record the following transactions for April using the financial statement effects template.

April transactions

    Required: Record each transaction in the template below. For every column a transaction affects, enter the amount on the first line and choose the account title on the second.

    Two habits make this quicker. Work left to right across one transaction before starting the next, and check after each one that the balance sheet still balances: the change in cash plus the change in noncash assets must equal the change in liabilities plus contributed capital plus earned capital. Revenues and expenses reach the balance sheet through earned capital, so any amount in the revenue or expense columns has a matching amount in earned capital.
