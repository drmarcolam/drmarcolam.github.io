Part B

Netting the gains and losses, and the tax on the preferential income

Put each item in the column for its character, then net. The net §1231 gain is treated as a long-term capital gain and joins the 15% group. A net short-term capital loss is applied against the long-term groups starting with the highest rate: 28% first, then 25%, then 15%. Qualified dividends are taxed at 15% and are not reduced by capital losses.
